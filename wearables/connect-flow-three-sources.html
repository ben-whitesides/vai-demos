One connect UI.
Three sources.

The same six-step VAI connect flow serves Apple Health, Health Connect and WHOOP. Scan any column top-to-bottom: steps 1–2 are pixel-identical — one VAI design. Steps 3–6 carry the per-source differences: each vendor's own un-restylable grant screen (step 4, not ours to design) and three different "did it work?" truth models (step 5).

Accent shown in your theme color (green here) — it inherits each user's custom profile theme, not a hardcoded color.

One shared design
Steps 1–2 are one UI, pixel-identical across all three sources. Steps 3 & 6 reuse a shared layout shell — only the body copy and manage actions swap per source.
The real per-source cost
Three vendor grant screens (step 4 — Apple's sheet, Android's permissions screen, WHOOP's hosted sign-in; none are ours to design) + three verify models (step 5) + per-source copy. Not three distinct VAI screen sets.
Three truth models at step 5
Apple never reports a read grant → probe the data, honest fallback. Health Connect reports granted permissions → deterministic confirm. WHOOP proves itself by the sign-in → approved scopes come back with it.
