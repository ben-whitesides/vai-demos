1What this covers

This document governs the health and fitness data you choose to connect to VAI through the VITALS feature. It sits alongside VAI's general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy; where this document is more specific about wearable health data, this document controls for that data.

VITALS is private by default. The data described here is visible only to you and AVANTI (VAI's AI coaching assistant). Coaches, scouts, teammates, and anyone viewing your public VAI profile do not see your VITALS data.

2What data we collect — by source and metric

VAI reads only the data types you approve at connection, from the source you connect. We request the minimum needed to power the readouts and coaching you see in the app. Per source:

SourceData types we may read (with your permission)
Apple Health (iOS)Heart rate & resting heart rate, heart-rate variability (SDNN), sleep analysis & stages, blood oxygen (SpO₂), respiratory rate, body temperature, blood pressure, blood glucose, VO₂max, workouts & workout route (GPS), active energy, distance, steps, and body measurements (weight, height, body fat) where present.
Google Health Connect (Android)The equivalent records: heart rate & resting HR, HRV (RMSSD), sleep sessions & stages, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, skin temperature, blood pressure, blood glucose, VO₂max, hydration, exercise sessions, distance, speed, elevation, active calories, and body measurements.
WHOOP (premium, optional)Recovery score, day strain, sleep performance/efficiency/consistency, HRV (RMSSD), resting HR, SpO₂, and skin temperature, via WHOOP's authorized API.

You see the exact data types requested on the permission screen at connection, and you can grant or deny each one. Some readouts require a paired wearable; where a data type isn't available, VITALS shows an honest "no data yet" state rather than an estimate. (Checklist items 2, 3.)

3How we use it

We use your connected health data only to provide the VITALS features you see in the app:

  • Display your readings, trends, and history in your chosen VITALS style.
  • Let AVANTI read your data (with your consent) to generate coaching for you — recovery and readiness guidance, suggested workouts, meal and macro suggestions, and progress encouragement. Scores such as Recovery, Readiness, Stress, Training Load, and Workout Grade are computed by VAI/AVANTI from your data; they are not provided by the wearable.
  • Keep a private history so AVANTI can show your progress over time.

This use is limited to improving your own health and fitness management and is only ever surfaced to you in the app. We do not use your health data for any purpose that isn't visible to you as a VITALS or AVANTI feature. (Checklist items 3, 5; Apple §5.1.3(i), Google G7.)

Third-party AI processing (important)

AVANTI's coaching is generated using an AI service operated on VAI's behalf via Amazon Web Services (AWS Bedrock). To produce your coaching, relevant health data may be processed by that AI service. That processing is performed solely to generate the coaching output shown to you in VAI, under VAI's control and contractual data protections; the data is not used to train third-party models for other purposes, and is not repurposed or resold. (Checklist item 5; Apple §5.1.2(i) third-party-AI disclosure. [counsel]: confirm the AI processor name/contract terms to disclose.)

4What we never do

Hard limits on your wearable data

  • We never use your wearable health data for advertising, marketing, or use-based data mining — for you or through any third party (ad networks, analytics vendors, or data brokers).
  • We never sell, license, lease, or otherwise transfer your wearable health data to any third party — including data brokers, ad platforms, resellers, or recruiters — even if you separately consent. (WHOOP prohibits this without exception; we apply that same rule to all sources.)
  • We never build or sell a health profile of you, and we do not attempt to re-identify de-identified data.
  • We never use your data for credit, lending, or insurance decisions.
  • VITALS is not a medical device and is not for high-risk or life-critical use, and VAI is not a children-exclusive service.

(Checklist item 4; Apple A4/A5/A9/A10, Google G5/G6, WHOOP W5.)

5Your consent

Before we read anything, you give affirmative, explicit consent by ticking the consent checkbox on the VITALS connect screen — immediately before the wearable's own permission screen. It is never pre-ticked and never bundled into general app terms. You then separately approve the specific data types on Apple's, Google's, or WHOOP's own permission screen.

Your consent is revocable at any time from VITALS settings ("Disconnect"), and from the wearable's own settings. Revoking never blocks unrelated paid features — connecting a wearable is not a precondition of any other VAI+ or Mentor function. (Checklist item 3; Apple A3, Google G4.)

7WHOOP — special handling

WHOOP's API terms prohibit keeping permanent copies of WHOOP data beyond its cache window. Accordingly, for WHOOP-sourced data VAI stores only derived daily summaries and trends — never raw WHOOP payloads. When a summary isn't enough, a "Refresh — get latest" control pulls live WHOOP data on demand, for display only; that live detail is not stored beyond WHOOP's cache window. WHOOP data is never sold or shared with any third party under any circumstances. (Checklist item 4; WHOOP W5, W7 §4.2(b).)

8Who can see your data

VITALS is private to you and AVANTI. VAI will not surface any wearable metric to another person — a teammate, coach, leaderboard, or shared view — without a separate, explicit opt-in from you, distinct from the connect-time consent. Today, VITALS has no such sharing. (Checklist item 8; WHOOP W6.)

9Security

Wearable-sourced data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-accepted standards, stored in VAI's own secured infrastructure. VAI does not store Apple Health–sourced personal health information in Apple iCloud. If VAI becomes aware of unauthorized access to your data, VAI will notify affected users (and WHOOP, for WHOOP data) as required. (Checklist items 7, 9; Apple A7, Google G8, WHOOP W8.)

10Legal & store compliance

  • VAI (Next Level Athlete LLC), the legal entity providing the service, is the submitter of record on the Apple App Store and Google Play for this health-data feature. (Item 10; Apple A8.)
  • This privacy policy is published at a live, publicly accessible, non-PDF URL, identical wherever it is linked (in-app, App Store Connect, Play Console, and the connect-time consent screen). (Item 1; Apple A1/A2, Google G2.)
  • VAI files Google Play's Health apps declaration, and completes WHOOP's App Approval review before scaling WHOOP access beyond WHOOP's small-user cap. (Items 12–13; Google G1, WHOOP W9.)
  • VAI does not use wearable data in any way that qualifies it as a HIPAA covered entity or a regulated medical device. (Item 14; Google G9.)
