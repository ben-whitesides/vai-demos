VAI · Wearables · Profile placement concept

VITALS on your profile.

Where it sits, how you turn it on, and how it looks next to STATS once enabled. The key idea: STATS is your public athletic identity; VITALS is your private physiological one — same neighborhood, opposite visibility. You set it up once in Profile Settings, pick your style family and sources, and it lives on your profile for your eyes (and AVANTI's) only.
